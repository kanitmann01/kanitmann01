Zero-Day Phishing Threat Intelligence Platform

NetSTAR Global · Jan – May 2026 · FastText, XGBoost/LightGBM, Power BI, Docker

A hybrid detector that combines a FastText text classifier, structured ML models, and distance rules to score brand-spoofing URLs before a client's users reach them. Trained on 1B+ phishing URLs; on a held-out split of 4,409 URLs it reached 96.7% accuracy and 99.3% precision, then shipped as an int8 edge model at roughly 5 ms p50 latency.

Unified Bharat: Cross-Sector Policy Lakehouse

Completed May 2026 · Spark, Iceberg, Docker

Five Indian government datasets (CSR spending, groundwater quality, institutions, district codes, population) reconciled on a Spark and Iceberg medallion architecture, then aggregated into a ~300-row state-year panel, a 99.88% reduction. Random Forest beat OLS and XGBoost at RMSE 0.811, evidence of a non-linear CSR and environment relationship. Code: unified-bharat.

Real-Time Twitch Analytics Pipeline

Jan 2026 · Kafka, Snowflake, dbt, Looker Studio

A producer polls the Twitch Helix API every 60 seconds for live AoE2 streams, Kafka buffers the events, Snowflake keeps the raw JSON, and dbt models it for a live Looker Studio dashboard. Code: twitch_stat_board.
